## 5. Your Privacy Rights

### 5.1 Access & Portability

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request your data anytime
- **Export**: Download in machine-readable format
- **Portability**: Transfer to another service
- **Transparency**: Understand how data is used

**How to Exercise:**
1. Go to Settings → Data & Privacy
2. Click "Export My Data"
3. Choose format (JSON/CSV/PDF)
4. Download your data

### 5.2 Correction & Deletion

You have the right to:
- **Correct**: Update inaccurate information
- **Delete**: Remove your account and data
- **Restrict**: Limit data processing
- **Object**: Opt-out of certain processing

**How to Delete Account:**
1. Settings → Account → Delete Account
2. Confirm deletion
3. 30-day recovery period
4. Permanently deleted after 30 days

### 5.3 Data Retention

We retain:
- **Active Accounts**: Indefinitely until deletion
- **Deleted Accounts**: 30-day recovery window
- **Backups**: 6 months
- **Logs**: 90 days
- **Activity History**: 1 year
- **Analytics**: Aggregated, indefinitely

### 5.4 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you're in EU, you have rights under GDPR:
- Right to access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object
- Rights related to automated decision-making

**GDPR Requests:** privacy@workplace-tools.example.com

### 5.5 CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you're in California, you have rights under CCPA:
- Right to know
- Right to delete
- Right to opt-out of sale
- Right to non-discrimination

**CCPA Requests:** privacy@workplace-tools.example.com
